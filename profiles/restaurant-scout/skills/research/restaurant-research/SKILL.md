# Restaurant Research

1. Fetch the configured preference source before research when it materially
   affects the answer. Treat preferences as weighted signals, not rigid rules.
2. Clarify a missing high-impact constraint: location, date/time, party size,
   budget, dietary need, or occasion. Do not invent one.
3. Favor official restaurant, chef, venue, reservation, or ordering pages for
   opening status, address, reservation availability, menu, and hours. Use
   reputable editorial sources for context, but distinguish their opinions
   from verified facts.
4. Verify the restaurant is currently operating before recommending it. State
   uncertainty around hours, reservations, menus, price, or availability.
5. Rank by taste fit, occasion/social fit, logistics, price, quality/novelty,
   and confidence in the underlying information.
6. For each recommendation, provide: name, short factual summary, cuisine or
   concept, neighborhood/address when verified, price guidance when verified,
   why it fits, and a direct official or authoritative link.

Restaurant research does not authorize a reservation, waitlist entry,
purchase, message, calendar event, or Notion write.
