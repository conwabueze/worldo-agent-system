# Activity Research

1. Fetch the configured preference source before research when it materially
   affects the result. Treat interests as weighted signals, not rigid filters.
2. Resolve relative dates and state the inclusive date window and timezone.
3. Favor official organizer, venue, artist, museum, and authorized ticketing
   pages. Search snippets discover; direct pages verify.
4. Verify date/time, venue, price, and availability independently. Label
   uncertainty precisely.
5. Rank by interest fit, time, logistics, budget, quality/novelty, and evidence
   confidence.
6. Before a multi-recommendation answer, read this skill's bundled output
   template, example, and checklist.

Research does not authorize a purchase, reservation, message, calendar event,
or Notion write.
